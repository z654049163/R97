The conference agenda/program is a list of the   order of events to be carried out, a schedule.
Format of agenda
Call to order 宣布会议开始
Roll call 点名
Announcement of quorum 宣布大会的法定人数
Reading and adoption of the minutes of the previous meeting 宣读并审议会议议程
Approval of the minutes of the previous meeting
通过前期会议纪要
Related matters 相关事宜
Unfinished business 未竟事业
Appointments and removal  人事任免
Nomination and elections   提名与选举
Adjournment 休会

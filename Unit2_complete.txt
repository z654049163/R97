=== Slide 12 ===
Learning Aids
①Two main business letter formats
●Full  block  format
This format  is  very  modern
Every  line  of  the  letter  begins  at  the  left margin(flush   left)
A  space   of  one   line   between   paragraphs.
Open   punctuation: no   punctuation   marks outside the  body  of the  letter,except for  a comma after the salutation and the complementary close respectively.

=== Slide 13 ===
Learning Aids
①Two main business letter formats
●Semi-block/ indented  format
The date and the complimentary  close  near  the  right  margin.
The  first  line  of  each paragraph is indented,with no space of one line between paragraphs.
If  the  letterhead  isn't  preprinted  on  the  stationery,you  should   put  it directly above the date.

=== Slide 14 ===
Learning Aids
②Explanation  of  invitation  letters
●Letterhead
It usually appears at  the  very  top  of  the  letter,either  in  the  left  corner,right  corner,or  at the  center.Wherever  you  put  it,be  sure  that  your  address  is  complete.
e.g.:
School of Law
Shandong University
Jinan,Shandong 250100
P.R.China

=== Slide 15 ===
Learning Aids
●Date
Put  down  the  full  date:day,month,and  year.It  comes   right  after  and just below your address.It is a common practice to put the date against the  left  margin  in the full  block  style.The date of the  letter should  be quite clear.The year should be given in full,the month should be written in words to avoid misunderstanding and confusion,and the day may be written in cardinal or  ordinal  numbers  and  may be put before(British style) or after (American  style) the  month.
British Style:8 May,2006      8th March,2006
American  Style:May  8,2006       March  8th,2004

=== Slide 16 ===
Learning Aids
Inside   address
Always on the left margin.
Courtesy title (e.g .Mrs., Ms.,Miss,Mr.), Professional  title(e.g.Dr.,Professor)
Business title (e.g.Manager, Director), Courtesy and  professional titles always appear  before the name while  business  titles  come  after  the  name.
Never  use  business titles  in  the  salutation.
Remember  to  make  the  inside  address  as complete as possible.

=== Slide 17 ===
Learning Aids
Salutation
This is a greeting to the recipient.
e.g.Dear Ms.Ann Mellon (use Ms. for a woman recipient when her marital status is not known or when she prefers Ms.),or Mr.John Wood,or Dear Professor Williams.
Salutation is also always on the left margin.

Signature

=== Slide 22 ===
Learning Aids
●Enclosure(s)(if any)
This can be used to remind the recipient of the additional material(s) put in the invitation letter, such as an agenda of a conference or a curriculum vitae in an application letter. Some prefer the abbreviation Encl.

=== Slide 23 ===
Learning Aids
③Tips  for  writing  an  invitation  letter 1/3
Make  sure  invitation  letters  are  cordial  and  enthusiastic.
Send  invitation  letters  out  with  ample  advance  notice.
Give  the  name  of  the   conference,the  time  and  the  venue  at  the beginning.
Do  not  use  abbreviations  in  formal  invitations  except  for  name titles such as Mr., Mrs.,Ms.and   Dr.

=== Slide 24 ===
Learning Aids
③Tips  for  writing  an  invitation  letter 2/3
(Optionally)Include  any  accommodations  that  will   be   made,such  as  lodging,  meals  and  transportation.
Give the  name of the contact  person along with  phone  numbers and address(including email).

=== Slide 25 ===
Learning Aids
③Tips  for  writing  an  invitation  letter 3/3
If you invite someone to speak at a conference/seminar,you should also  mention the type of speech,the topic or the scope and how long the speech will be.
If  a  reply  is  required,place  R.S.V.P.(Please  reply)at  the  end  of  the invitation,often at the  left  corner.
Check your letter for typos or any other mistakes in language,such as grammar,spelling,structure,etc.
Keep the letter concise,factual,and to the point with a polite tone.And use language and terminology familiar to the intended recipients.

=== Slide 26 ===
A letter of acceptance is to inform the conference host/organizer of your acceptance of the invitation and attendance at the conference.
A letter of refusal is written if you cannot accept the invitation for one reason or another. You should politely and promptly write to the organizer to decline the invitation. By doing so, you allow them time for arranging backup plans.

=== Slide 30 ===
Tips for Letter Writing
Good letter writing includes proper grammar, punctuation, capitalization, format, structure and addressing persons of title.
Be consistent in format.
Be brief, succinct, and unambiguous in content.
Be courteous and polite in tone.

=== Slide 31 ===
Learning Aids
①Tips for writing a letter of acceptance
Your thanks are  usually expressed at the very  beginning of the  letter, but sometimes they can also be placed at the end of the first paragraph.
An acceptance should be brief.It is considered to be good to simply state your acceptance and briefly mention the main contents of the invitation.

=== Slide 32 ===
Learning Aids
If  necessary,inquire  about   particular   information  such  as  accommodations  during  the  conference,equipment  for  your  speech  or presentation at the conference/seminar,etc.
Once  you   agree  to   accept  the   invitation,try  to   avoid   ungracious  amplifications.For example,"I am very busy,but I can manage it or I will come if the situation permits."

=== Slide 33 ===
Learning Aids
②Tips for writing a letter of refusal
Make a courteous,sincere  remark,and  present convincing  reason(s)for declining the invitation.
Suggest  any  alternative  courses  of  action  or  other  resources,if appropriate.
Express good wishes for the next cooperation in the future.

Subject: the focus/topic of the email
Body: purpose in writing the email, details, etc

=== Slide 37 ===
Learning Aids
Tips for writing an effective email
Start with thank-you remarks or your reason(s)for writing the email.
Create  a  good  subject  line.The  subject  line  states  the  focus  of  your topic in your email.
Identify  the  purpose  of  your  message.
Place your most important information in the first paragraph,and even get the well-thought details packed into the first sentence or two.
Keep your message clear and concise.
Be careful when sending attachments.

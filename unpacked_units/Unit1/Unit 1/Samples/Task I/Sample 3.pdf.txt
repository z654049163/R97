ICWMC 2006 addresses wireless related topics (techn ologies, networking, 
applications) concerning integration of latest technological advances to realize mobile 
and ubiquitous service environments for advanced ap plications and services in 
wireless networks. Mobility and wireless, special s ervices and lessons learnt from 
particular deployment complement the traditional wireless topics.

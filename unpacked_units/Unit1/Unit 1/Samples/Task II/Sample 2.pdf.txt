6th Global Forum on Reinventing Government 
 
The government of the Republic of Korea, in partner ship with the United 
Nations, will be hosting the 6 th Global Forum on Reinventing Government to be held 
in Seoul from May 24-27, 2005. On behalf of the Kor ean Government, I am pleased 
to invite you to this meaningful event. 
The first of its series to convene in Asia, the 6 th Forum will focus on the 
overarching theme “Toward Participatory and Transpa rent Governance.” Attending 
the Forum will be representatives of 191 Member Sta tes of the United Nations and 
renowned experts from international organizations, business, academia and civil 
society. 
The Korean Government and the United Nations have b een working closely to 
organize the 6 th Forum, aiming to make it a great success. Looking forward to seeing 
you in the dynamic capital of Korea, I cordially invited you to the vibrant exchange of 
ideas and experiences at the Forum, which will sure ly generate fresh momentum for 
opportunity for participants to strengthen cooperation for building good governance at 
home and for reaching the Millennium Development Goals in the 21st century. 
better governance across the globe.

Registration  
Participation is open to government officials, acad emics and representatives of 
international organizations (such as the UN, OECD 2, World Bank, and etc.), business 
and civil society and etc. There is no register fee  for participants to attend the 6 th 
Global Forum. However, registration fee does not in clude accommodations. 
Prospective participants are kindly requested to re gister in advance by registering 
online at www.6thglobalforum.org or by submitting the enclosed registration form to  
the Secretariat for the 6th Global Forum by mail or fax. 
 
Accommodation 
The Secretariat for the 6 th Global Forum has selected hotels with special disc ount 
rates for participants in the vicinity of COEX 3, the conference venue. Hotel 
reservations should be made through the Official Housing Bureau. All reservations for 
participants are on a first-come, first-served basi s. For more details, please visit our 
website at www.6thglobalforum.org.
